# Editor Workflow Tools

This page gives an editor's view of Superdesk. Each task says what you do and where, and links to the page with the full steps. Many actions need a privilege. If one is missing, ask your administrator.

## Watch the desks

* **Follow your desk's workflow.** Open **Monitoring** to see each stage of the selected desk as a group. Click a group's name to work with that group only. See [Monitoring Tab](../workspace-and-search/monitoring-tab.md#groups-in-the-monitoring-view).
* **Watch several desks at once.** Create a custom workspace that combines stages and saved searches from the desks you look after. See [Desks and Custom Workspaces](../workspace-and-search/desks-and-custom-workspaces.md#creating-a-custom-workspace).
* **Show a saved search in Monitoring.** Follow a topic across desks as its own group. See [Searching Content in Superdesk](../workspace-and-search/searching-content-in-superdesk.md#saved-searches-in-monitoring).
* **See the whole newsroom.** Open **Master Desk** from the Workspace Panel for item counts per stage on every desk and what each user is working on. See [Master Desk](../workspace-and-search/master-desk.md#overview-tab).

## Take and review items

* **Take an item from an incoming stage.** Double-click it in Monitoring to open it for editing. It is locked to you while you work. See [Monitoring Tab](../workspace-and-search/monitoring-tab.md#opening-an-item).
* **Get into a locked item.** Ask the person who has it open to close it. With the **Unlock content** privilege, you can click **Unlock** instead. See [Creating and Editing Content Items](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/README.md#locks).
* **Review suggestions.** Click a suggestion in the body and choose **Accept** or **Reject**. All suggestions must be resolved before the item can be published. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#accepting-or-rejecting-a-suggestion).
* **Answer comments.** Read, reply to and resolve inline comments in the body or in the **Inline comments** widget. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#reading-replying-to-and-resolving-inline-comments).
* **See what changed.** Use the **Versions/History** widget or **Compare versions**. See [Creating and Editing Content Items](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/README.md#versions-and-history).
* **Mark an item for a user.** Open the item's action menu (three vertical dots) and choose **Mark for user**. **Mark and send** also moves the item to the next stage. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#mark-for-user).
* **Send an item back.** Use the **Send to** tab of the **Send to / Publish** panel to return the item to a reporter's desk and stage. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#sending-an-item-to-a-desk-or-stage).

## Publish

* **Publish an item.** Click **Send to / Publish** in the top bar and use the **Publish** tab. Under **From** you can publish from another desk. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#publishing-an-item).
* **Publish several items.** Select them in Monitoring or Search and click **Publish** in the action bar. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#publishing-several-items-at-once).
* **Embargo or schedule an item.** Set **Embargo** or **Publish schedule** in the **Publish** tab. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#embargo-and-publish-schedule).
* **Cancel a scheduled publication.** Deschedule the item to bring it back into the workflow. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#descheduling-items).
* **Publish from the top bar.** Buttons such as **Publish** and **Publish & Continue** save a step when your administrator has added them. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#shortcut-buttons-in-the-top-bar).

## After publication

* **Choose the right action.** Update, correct, kill, take down, unpublish and resend each affect subscribers differently. See [Updates, corrections, kills and takedowns](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/README.md#choosing-the-right-action).
* **Correct a published item.** Open it and click **Correct** in the top bar. If your newsroom uses the corrections workflow, the correction goes through a desk before it is sent. See [Corrections](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/corrections.md#correcting-a-published-item), and its section [The corrections workflow](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/corrections.md#the-corrections-workflow).
* **Kill or take down an item.** Use **Kill** or **Takedown** when subscribers must stop using an item. Both are final. See [Kills, takedowns and unpublishing](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/kills-takedowns-and-unpublishing.md#killing-an-item), and its section [Taking down an item](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/kills-takedowns-and-unpublishing.md#taking-down-an-item).
* **Withdraw an item for now.** **Unpublish** returns the item to the workflow so it can be published again. See [Kills, takedowns and unpublishing](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/kills-takedowns-and-unpublishing.md#unpublishing-an-item).
* **Resend an item.** Choose **Publishing actions** > **Resend item** in a list to send the same version to subscribers you pick. See [Kills, takedowns and unpublishing](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/kills-takedowns-and-unpublishing.md#resending-an-item).
* **Check that it went out.** The **Publish Queue** shows each transmission to each subscriber and its status. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#checking-the-publish-queue).

## Curate content

* **Highlights.** Add items to a highlight list, for example the top stories of the day, and publish it as a unit. See [Highlights](../workspace-and-search/highlights.md).
* **Packages.** Group related items, such as a story and its pictures, so they are published together. See [Packages](../content-management/packages.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Content lists and Planning are optional modules. If you do not see them, ask your administrator.
{% endhint %}

* **Content lists.** Build an ordered list of articles for your website, such as the homepage top stories. See [Content lists](../integrations-and-advanced-features/content-lists.md#creating-a-list).
* **Assign coverage.** In Planning, assign a coverage to a desk or a person. See [Planning items and coverages](../superdesk-planning-component/planning-items-and-coverages.md#assigning-a-coverage).
* **Follow your desk's assignments.** Use **Desk Assignments** in the Assignments workspace, or the **Assignments** tab in Master Desk. See [Assignments](../superdesk-planning-component/assignments.md#the-assignments-workspace) and [Master Desk](../workspace-and-search/master-desk.md#assignments-tab).
* **Pick the featured stories.** Choose and order the day's most important planning items. See [Planning items and coverages](../superdesk-planning-component/planning-items-and-coverages.md#featured-stories).

## Run the desk

* **Close a desk.** Use the **Desk Router** widget on the desk's Dashboard to close it and route its incoming content to another desk. Your administrator sets up routing first. See [Dashboards and Widgets](../workspace-and-search/dashboards-and-widgets.md#desk-router) and [Desk Creation and Desk Management](../administration/desk-creation-and-desk-management.md#desk-routing).
* **Recognise a closed desk.** Closed desks are shown in red in the Desks menu, and the top bar says where their content goes. See [Desks and Custom Workspaces](../workspace-and-search/desks-and-custom-workspaces.md#closed-desks).
* **Tell everyone something.** If your role allows it, post a system message from **Admin tools** > **System Message** in the Main menu. It appears at the top of every user's screen. See [System messages](../administration/system-messages.md#creating-a-message).
