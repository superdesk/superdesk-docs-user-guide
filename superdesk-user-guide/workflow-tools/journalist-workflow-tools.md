# Journalist Workflow Tools

This page follows a reporter through a typical day in Superdesk. Each task says what you do and where, and links to the page with the full steps. What you see depends on your role and your newsroom's setup. If something is missing, ask your administrator.

## Start your day

* **Pick your desk.** Click the **Desks menu** at the top left of the screen and choose the desk you work on today. Monitoring and the Dashboard follow this choice. See [Navigation](../navigating-superdesk/navigation.md#the-desks-menu).
* **See your stages.** Click the **Monitoring** icon in the Workspace Panel. Each group in Monitoring is a stage of your desk, a desk output or a saved search. See [Monitoring Tab](../workspace-and-search/monitoring-tab.md#groups-in-the-monitoring-view).
* **Work across desks.** If you write for more than one desk, create a custom workspace that shows the stages you need in one view. See [Desks and Custom Workspaces](../workspace-and-search/desks-and-custom-workspaces.md#creating-a-custom-workspace).
* **Check what is marked for you.** Colleagues can mark items for you. Click **Marked for me** in the top bar, or the **Marked for me** button in the Monitoring toolbar. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#finding-items-marked-for-you).
* **Check your assignments.** If your newsroom uses Planning, click the **Assignments** icon in the Workspace Panel and choose **My Assignments**. See [Assignments](../superdesk-planning-component/assignments.md#the-assignments-workspace).

## Write a story

* **Create an item.** Click the **Create** button in the Monitoring header, or **Create new** in the bar at the bottom of the screen, and pick a template. The item is created on the desk you have selected. See [Creating and Editing Content Items](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/README.md#creating-an-item).
* **Start from an assignment.** In the Assignments workspace, choose **Start Working** on an assignment. The new article is linked to it. See [Assignments](../superdesk-planning-component/assignments.md#working-on-an-assignment).
* **Write and format the body.** Use the toolbar above the body for headings, lists, links, tables and embeds. See [Writing and formatting](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/writing-and-formatting.md#writing-in-the-body-editor).
* **Add pictures, video and audio.** Click **Media** in the body toolbar, or drop files into the body. See [Edit Media items](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/edit-media-items.md#adding-media-to-the-body).
* **Check spelling.** Misspelled words are underlined while you type. Right-click a word for suggestions. See [Writing and formatting](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/writing-and-formatting.md#checking-spelling).
* **Save your work.** Click **Save** in the top bar. Autosave protects your changes, but colleagues only see the versions you save. See [Creating and Editing Content Items](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/README.md#saving-autosave-and-locks).

## Work with colleagues

* **Leave a comment.** Open the **Comments** widget in the widget bar and type `@` to mention a colleague or a desk. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#comments).
* **Comment on a sentence.** Select text in the body and click **Comment** in the toolbar. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#inline-comments).
* **Suggest changes.** Turn on **Toggle Suggestions Mode** in the body toolbar so your edits are kept as suggestions. An item with open suggestions cannot be published. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#suggestions).
* **Mark an item for someone.** Open the item's action menu (three vertical dots) and choose **Mark for user** to bring it to a colleague's attention. See [Collaboration tools](../content-management/creating-and-editing-content-items/collaboration-tools.md#mark-for-user).

## Send your story on

* **Send it to a desk or stage.** Click **Send to / Publish** in the top bar, go to the **Send to** tab, and pick the desk and stage. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#sending-an-item-to-a-desk-or-stage).
* **Publish it, if your role allows.** Use the **Publish** tab of the same panel. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#publishing-an-item).
* **Set an embargo or a publish schedule.** Enter the date and time in the **Publish** tab before you publish. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#embargo-and-publish-schedule).
* **Use the top bar buttons.** Your administrator may have added buttons such as **To Desk** or **Publish** to the top bar. See [Sending, scheduling and publishing items](../content-management/sending-scheduling-and-publishing.md#shortcut-buttons-in-the-top-bar).

## After your story is published

* **Update your story.** Open the published story and click **Update** in the top bar. Superdesk creates a new story linked to the original. See [Updates](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/updates.md).
* **Fix a mistake.** Check which action fits, update or correction, before you start. See [Updates, corrections, kills and takedowns](../content-management/updates-corrections-kills-and-takedowns/README.md#choosing-the-right-action).
* **Translate it.** In a list, open the item's actions menu and choose **Translate** and a language. The translation is created on the desk you have selected. See [Translating content](../content-management/translating-content.md#translating-an-item).

## Spike and unspike

* **Spike an item you no longer need.** Open the item's action menu (three vertical dots) and choose **Spike Item**. Spiking from your Personal space deletes the item for good. See [Spiked Items](../content-management/spiked-items.md#spiking-an-item).
* **Bring it back.** Open **Spiked Items** in the Workspace Panel and unspike the item to a desk and stage. See [Spiked Items](../content-management/spiked-items.md#unspiking-an-item).

## Find content

* **Search Superdesk.** Click the **Search** icon in the Workspace Panel to search all content you can access. See [Searching Content in Superdesk](../workspace-and-search/searching-content-in-superdesk.md#opening-search).
* **Save a search you run often.** Saved searches can be run again, shown in Monitoring and sent to you by email. See [Searching Content in Superdesk](../workspace-and-search/searching-content-in-superdesk.md#saved-searches).

## Your own space and settings

* **Draft in private.** Items in your Personal space are visible only to you until you send them to a desk. See [Desks and Custom Workspaces](../workspace-and-search/desks-and-custom-workspaces.md#personal-space).
* **Learn the keyboard shortcuts.** Press and hold **Alt+K** to see them. See [Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).
* **Set up your profile.** Click your avatar in the top-right corner and choose **Manage profile**. See [Your profile and preferences](../navigating-superdesk/your-profile-and-preferences.md#your-profile-page).
* **Choose your default desk and notifications.** Both are in your profile. See [Your profile and preferences](../navigating-superdesk/your-profile-and-preferences.md#default-desk) for the default desk, and its [Notifications](../navigating-superdesk/your-profile-and-preferences.md#notifications) section.
